3-Day curriculum

Program schedule

Each session is 3 hours of structured learning - concept introduction, live industrial demo, hands-on exercises, and Q&A.

Day 1

Smart Automation & Robotics

Cobots · AGVs · AMRs · Motion Systems

+
Session 1 · 3 hours Delivered by: Metronelec · MiR · Universal Robots

Robots and AGV/AMR in Factories

  • Introduction to industrial robotics and automation
  • Collaborative robots (cobots), applications and programming
  • AGV vs AMR: concepts, differences, and use cases
  • Live demo: robot programming and mobile robot navigation
  • Use cases and deployments in Moroccan industry
Session 2 · 3 hours Delivered by: igus

Advanced Motion Systems (igus 4.0)

  • Introduction to smart motion plastics
  • Low-cost automation solutions for SMEs and large manufacturers
  • Digital twin and predictive maintenance concepts
  • Hands-on: configuring motion systems
  • Applications in flexible manufacturing environments
Day 2

Connectivity & Smart Manufacturing Systems

IIoT · MES · IO-Link · OEE · Smart Sensors

+
Session 3 · 3 hours Delivered by: Evocon

IIoT and MES in Manufacturing

  • Introduction to Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT)
  • Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES) fundamentals
  • Real-time production monitoring and data collection
  • Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE) calculation and improvement
  • Demo: data collection and visualization platform
Session 4 · 3 hours Delivered by: ifm electronic

IO-Link Technology for IIoT

  • Smart sensors and industrial connectivity overview
  • IO-Link architecture and communication protocol
  • Integration with PLCs and cloud-based systems
  • Hands-on: sensor configuration and remote diagnostics
  • Use cases in predictive maintenance and quality control
Day 3

Strategy & Industry 4.0 Transformation

SIRI Framework · Roadmaps · ROI · Group Workshop

+
Session 5 · 3 hours Delivered by: INCIT

SIRI Assessment Framework

  • Introduction to the Smart Industry Readiness Index (SIRI)
  • Key dimensions: Process, Technology, and Organisation
  • Assessment methodology and scoring
  • Case studies from ASEAN and global manufacturers
  • Understanding where companies stand on the I4.0 journey
Session 6 · 3 hours Delivered by: INCIT

From Assessment to Deployment

  • Building Industry 4.0 transformation roadmaps
  • Prioritising initiatives by impact and feasibility
  • ROI modelling and implementation strategies
  • Group workshop: mock SIRI assessment on a Moroccan case
  • Team roadmap design and peer presentation

Bonus

🏭 Industrial Field Trip

A half-day guided visit to an active industrial facility in the Fes-Meknes region. Observe Industry 4.0 technologies deployed at scale in a real Moroccan factory. Included in the registration fee.
